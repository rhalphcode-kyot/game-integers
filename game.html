📖 Instructions

Welcome to Integer Quest!

  • There are 5 main levels with 110 questions in total.
  • Level 1: 20 Easy items.
  • Level 2: 20 Easy items.
  • Level 3: 20 Moderate items.
  • Level 4: 20 Moderate items.
  • Level 5: 30 Hard items.
  • You have 3 lives for the main quest.
  • Each question has a 15-second timer.
  • Correct answer: +10 points.
  • Wrong answer or timeout: -1 life.
  • Your progress is automatically saved after every question.
  • An optional Bonus Round is separate and has no life penalty.
